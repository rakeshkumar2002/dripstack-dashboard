DripStack

A Technical Lifecycle Communication Platform — event-triggered, branching drip sequences of technical communications to technicians during incident resolution and onboarding.

Your product sends DripStack an event — an equipment fault, an API error, a Sentry issue. DripStack matches it to a sequence and holds a multi-step, branching conversation with the technician on your behalf: a plain-language explanation of what went wrong, the code and logs formatted so they are actually readable, and action buttons (resolve / I need help) — over email, Slack, and Teams. It follows up, and escalates if nobody responds.

New here? The Integration guide covers the whole thing in two API calls, with copyable code and a live signature tool.

What's inside

Conversations that survive

A sequence can wait hours or days for a reply without losing its place, and a button click always beats a pending timeout.

Delivered where they are

Email, Slack and Teams, plus signed callbacks into your own systems and single sign-on for your team.

Separated by organization

Your data is only ever visible to your organization, with per-role permissions and an append-only record of who did what.

Safe links

Every action link is signed and tied to one incident, and opening one never changes anything on its own.
